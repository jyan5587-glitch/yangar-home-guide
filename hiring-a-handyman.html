How to Hire a Handyman

Questions to ask before you hire anyone, including us.

Before you hire

  • Ask for proof of liability insurance.
  • Get a written scope and quote.
  • Ask how they handle work that needs a licensed trade.
  • Ask for references or photos of past work.

Red flags

  • Large upfront payments in cash.
  • No written scope or vague pricing.
  • Offering electrical or plumbing work without a license.

After the job

  • Walk the work together before final payment.
  • Keep receipts and photos for your records.
