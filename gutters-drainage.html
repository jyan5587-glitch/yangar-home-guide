Gutters and Drainage

Water moving the wrong way causes much of the damage we see, especially on expansive clay soil.

Gutters

  • Clean at least twice a year, more under oak and pecan trees.
  • Check that downspouts discharge 4–6 ft from the foundation.
  • Look for sagging sections and loose hangers.

Drainage

  • Soil should slope away from the house.
  • Watch for standing water near the slab after rain.
  • Keep flower beds from trapping water against siding.

Warning signs

  • Stains on fascia or soffits.
  • Cracks in drywall above doors and windows (can signal movement; consult a foundation pro).
  • Mildew on exterior walls.
