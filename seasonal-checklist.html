Seasonal Maintenance Checklist

A season-by-season list for San Antonio and Bulverde homes.

Spring (Mar–May)

  • Clean gutters and downspouts after oak pollen and leaf drop.
  • Check window and door caulking before summer heat.
  • Walk the roof line from the ground for missing shingles after storms.
  • Test smoke and CO alarms; replace batteries.

Summer (Jun–Sep)

  • Change HVAC filters monthly in peak heat.
  • Keep the outdoor AC unit clear of plants and debris (2 ft).
  • Watch for foundation gaps along the slab as clay soil dries.
  • Check irrigation for leaks and follow local watering rules.

Fall (Oct–Nov)

  • Clean gutters again after leaf drop.
  • Insulate exposed hose bibs and outdoor pipes before the first freeze.
  • Disconnect garden hoses.
  • Check weatherstripping on exterior doors.

Winter (Dec–Feb)

  • Know where your main water shut-off is.
  • Keep a freeze kit: faucet covers, pipe insulation, flashlight.
  • Check attic for pests and insulation gaps.
  • Plan spring repairs early, before contractor calendars fill.
