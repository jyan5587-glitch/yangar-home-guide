Small Repairs to Fix Before a Sale or Lease

Small fixes that make a good first impression.

Walls and paint

  • Patch holes and dings.
  • Touch up or repaint in neutral colors.
  • Caulk around tubs, sinks, and trim.

Doors and hardware

  • Fix sticking doors and loose hinges.
  • Replace worn or mismatched knobs and cabinet pulls.
  • Adjust closet and cabinet doors.

Finishing touches

  • Replace damaged blinds and window screens.
  • Fix or replace damaged baseboards and trim.
  • Pressure wash entry areas and walkways.
  • Repair fence boards and gates.
