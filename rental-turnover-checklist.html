Rental Turnover Checklist

Get a unit rent-ready quickly between tenants.

Walkthrough

  • Photograph every room at move-out, with dates.
  • Compare against the move-in condition report.
  • List damage beyond normal wear and tear.

Make-ready

  • Patch nail holes and touch up or repaint walls.
  • Clean or replace carpet; deep clean hard floors.
  • Replace burned-out bulbs and HVAC filter.
  • Check doors, locks, blinds, and window screens.
  • Rekey locks between tenants. Texas law requires the landlord, at the landlord's expense, to rekey security devices operated by a key, card, or combination no later than the seventh day after each tenant turnover date (closet and other interior door locks are excluded). Source: Texas Property Code §92.156.

Final check

  • Test smoke alarms.
  • Run every faucet and flush toilets to spot leaks (call a licensed plumber for repairs).
  • Final clean and fresh photos for the listing.
