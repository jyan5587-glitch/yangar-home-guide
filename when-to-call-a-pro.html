When to Call a Pro

Know what's DIY, what's handyman work, and what needs a licensed trade.

Usually DIY

  • Changing HVAC filters and light bulbs.
  • Testing smoke alarms.
  • Basic cleaning and gutter checks from the ground.

Good handyman work

  • Drywall patching, painting, and caulking.
  • Doors, locks, trim, cabinets, and blinds.
  • Fence repair and small carpentry.

Needs a licensed trade in Texas

  • Electrical work (TDLR-licensed electrician).
  • Plumbing, including faucets, toilets, and water heaters (TSBPE-licensed plumber).
  • AC and heating system repair (TDLR-licensed HVAC contractor).
  • Structural or foundation work (qualified contractor or engineer).
