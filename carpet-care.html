Carpet Care

Make carpet last longer between replacements, especially in rentals.

Routine

  • Vacuum high-traffic areas weekly.
  • Blot spills right away; don't rub.
  • Use doormats at every entry to cut Texas dust and grit.

Periodic

  • Deep clean every 12–18 months, and at each rental turnover.
  • Move furniture occasionally to even out wear.
  • Watch for rippling, which can mean it needs restretching.

Repair or replace?

  • Small burns, snags, and seams can often be patched.
  • Persistent odors, pad damage, or widespread stains usually mean replacement.
