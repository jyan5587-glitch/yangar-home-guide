Water Heater Basics

What owners can watch for. Water heater repair and replacement is plumbing work in Texas and needs a licensed plumber.

Know your unit

  • Find the age on the label; many tank units last about 8–12 years.
  • Know whether it's gas or electric.
  • Keep the area around it clear.

Watch for

  • Rust-colored water or rust on the tank.
  • Rumbling or popping noises.
  • Moisture or puddles at the base.
  • Water not as hot as it used to be.

What to do

  • Call a licensed plumber for leaks, valve issues, flushing, or replacement.
  • If it's leaking badly, shut off the cold water supply to the heater and power or gas, if safe.
  • Plan replacement before failure on older units.
