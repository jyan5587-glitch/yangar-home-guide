Preventing Burst Pipes in a Freeze

Central Texas freezes are rare but costly, as the February 2021 storm showed.

Before a freeze

  • Insulate exposed pipes in attics, garages, and exterior walls.
  • Cover outdoor hose bibs and disconnect hoses.
  • Find and test your main water shut-off valve.
  • Open cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls.

During a freeze

  • Let indoor faucets drip slowly.
  • Keep the thermostat steady, even overnight.
  • For rentals, remind tenants of these steps in writing.

If a pipe bursts

  • Shut off the main water valve right away.
  • Turn off electricity in affected areas if water is near outlets, only if safe.
  • Call a licensed plumber for the pipe repair.
  • Document damage with photos for insurance; drywall and cleanup can follow.
