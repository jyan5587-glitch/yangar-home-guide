HVAC Filter Care and AC Prep for Texas Heat

Simple upkeep homeowners can do themselves. Repairs, refrigerant, and system work belong to a TDLR-licensed air conditioning contractor.

Filters

  • Check the filter monthly in summer; replace when it looks gray or dusty.
  • Match the size printed on the old filter's frame.
  • Note the airflow arrow and point it toward the unit.
  • Write the change date on the filter edge.

Before summer

  • Clear leaves, grass, and plants from around the outdoor unit.
  • Make sure supply and return vents aren't blocked by furniture.
  • Check that the condensate drain line isn't visibly backed up or leaking.
  • Schedule a tune-up with a licensed HVAC contractor.

Call a licensed HVAC pro if

  • The system blows warm air or runs constantly.
  • You see ice on lines or the coil.
  • There's a burning smell, buzzing, or tripped breakers.
  • Water is pooling around the indoor unit.
